Download this PC’s certificate

01

This lets your phone trust the secure connection to your printing PC, which is needed for live camera access. Download it while connected to the same Wi-Fi as that PC.

Download certificate
Verify the certificate

Compare this SHA-256 fingerprint with the camera setup page opened on your Windows PC.

Install it on your Android phone

02
  1. Open Android Settings and search for CA certificate.
  2. Open Install a certificate → CA certificate. This is usually under Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials.
  3. Confirm the installation prompt, unlock your phone if asked, and choose the certificate you downloaded.

Menu names vary by phone. Choose CA certificate, rather than Wi-Fi certificate. Android asks you to confirm trust in certificates issued by your printing PC; only install the certificate from your own PC.

You can later remove this certificate from Android’s Trusted credentials → User section.

Open live scanning

03

After installing the certificate, open the secure receipt page below. Your saved receipt and pairing will be copied to it. Your original draft stays at the old address.

On the receipt page, tap Scan barcode and allow camera access. If Chrome shows a certificate warning, return to step 2 and check the certificate installation.
